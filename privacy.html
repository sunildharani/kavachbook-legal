KavachBook privacy policy

Effective 16 October 2026

KavachBook is a local-first Android app for organizing products, coverage, receipts, documents, service history, and reminders. Contact support@kavachbook.com with privacy questions.

Information used by the app

When you choose Google sign-in, the app receives your Google account identifier, email address, and display name to create or reopen a local KavachBook profile. We do not receive or store your Google password. Google processes sign-in under its own policies.

Information you enter or attach, including product details, serial numbers, purchase dates, warranty and insurance details, costs, notes, receipts, photographs, documents, and service records, is stored on your device. Receipt image recognition and PDF text extraction run on the device. The app uses files and the camera only when you choose or capture an item.

Storage, sharing, and permissions

This version has no KavachBook cloud account or server sync. We do not receive your records or attachments on a KavachBook server and cannot recover them from one. The app uses Android notification permission to show reminders you enable. Notification text may be visible according to your device's lock-screen settings; you can disable notifications in Android settings.

When you choose Back up to Google Drive, KavachBook requests access to Drive files it creates and uploads a ZIP containing your products, coverage, service records, reminders, and attachments to your Google Drive. It checks the uploaded file's size and checksum before reporting success. You can also save a ZIP to a location you choose in Android Files, such as Downloads. The ZIP is not password protected. Restoring a complete ZIP adds copies of its records and attachments to the device; it does not replace existing records. The app recognizes an exact ZIP already restored to the same local account and asks before adding another copy. Drive backups and locally saved ZIPs remain in their destinations when you delete local app data; manage those copies in Google Drive or Android Files. Android or Google device backup settings may separately affect app data. Google sign-in and Drive handle information under Google's policies. This version has no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting service integrated by KavachBook.

Retention and deletion

Local profile data and product records remain on the device until you delete them. In Settings, “Delete local data” removes the current profile's products and attachments while keeping the local profile. “Delete account and data” removes the current local profile and its records and managed attachments. Deletion does not delete your Google account or copies you exported or shared elsewhere. If an attachment cannot be removed immediately, the app keeps a local cleanup request and retries it. Learn about deletion or request help on the account deletion page.

Security and changes

App records are stored in Android app storage and managed attachments are kept in app-controlled storage. Protect your device and any exported backup files. We will update this policy before introducing server sync or changing how data is handled. The effective date above will change when the policy changes.
