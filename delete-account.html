KavachBook · Privacy policy

Delete your KavachBook account and data

KavachBook currently keeps your app account and vault on your device. We do not have a server copy of your products, documents, or local profile.

Delete in the app

Open KavachBook → Settings → Delete account and data → confirm. This removes the local profile, products, coverage, reminders, service history, and managed attachments for the signed-in account. You can also choose “Delete local data” to keep the profile while removing its product records.

Request help without the app

If you cannot open the app, email us to request deletion assistance or to ask whether we hold any account data. Please state that your request concerns KavachBook account deletion. We will reply with the steps possible for your situation. Because vault data is stored only on your device, we cannot erase device files remotely.

Request account deletion help

Deleting the KavachBook local profile does not delete your Google account or any ZIP/CSV backup you previously exported. See the privacy policy for details.
